## Automated results

- Recorded focused plain-JVM run passed 10 tests. Robolectric-dependent tests were excluded and are not claimed as passing.
- Recorded `:smarttubetv:assembleStbetaDebug` build completed successfully, producing the ARMv7 and universal APKs.

## Direct device observations

- The ARMv7 APK ran on the TCL Android TV. The three Local Library videos displayed picture. Timeline and D-pad seeking, pause, resume, completion, and return to the library were observed.
- Repeated playback created and released the app-owned local stream listener. No app-owned listener remained after leaving playback.
- Ordinary YouTube playback displayed picture after local playback.
- The TV-side HTTP request/response trace was not captured. HTTP range headers, `Content-Range`, and returned bytes therefore remain unverified.
